Why Revive?

Fort Wayne
was missing
the experience.

Because of work, I trained in gyms across the country — and the best ones all had something in common. Better equipment. Stronger culture. Recovery built in. Communities that felt bigger than showing up and lifting. And every flight back to Fort Wayne, the same realization: we don't have this.

So I built it.

"Fort Wayne had gyms. But it didn't have a place that combined serious training, premium recovery, and real community — the kind of experience you usually only find in top-tier facilities."

/01

The Word.

— Re·vive (verb) —

Revive means a second chance.

  • A second chance to get back to your routine.
  • A second chance to rebuild your discipline.
  • A second chance to feel stronger.
  • A second chance to feel healthier.
  • A second chance to feel in control again.

The name picked itself. Because that's what people kept telling me they were looking for. Not another membership. A reset. A way back. A place that made the work feel like it mattered again.
