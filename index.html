REVIVE means a second chance. To rebuild your discipline. To return to your routine. To feel stronger, healthier, and more in control. We didn't build another gym — we built the place Fort Wayne has been waiting for. The kind you usually only find in New York, Austin, or Miami.

002 — The Pillars

Built on three things.
Done right.

01 — Train  ·  02 — Recover  ·  03 — Belong

/01

Train Hard.

Top-tier strength platforms, dedicated turf for sled work and conditioning, and a layout designed for people who actually lift. No mirrors-and-attitude box gym. A facility built around output.

  • Olympic platforms + competition racks
  • Pendulum, Hammer Strength, Eleiko
  • 30-yard turf strip + sled track
  • Performance conditioning floor
/02

Recover Harder.

Recovery isn't optional — it is the work. Cold plunge, full-spectrum sauna, red-light therapy, and pneumatic compression — built into the experience, bookable from your phone.

  • 3 cold plunges (38–42°F)
  • Cedar dry sauna + infrared
  • Medical-grade red light beds
  • Normatec compression suites
/03

Belong Here.

A community of people who train. Weekly run club. Member events. Programmed group classes that move you forward — not the same playlist on a treadmill. Discipline becomes easier when the room expects it.

  • Saturday Run Club — free for everyone
  • Monthly member nights + socials
  • Strength, Hyrox, Pilates classes
  • The Founding 100 — a real cohort
