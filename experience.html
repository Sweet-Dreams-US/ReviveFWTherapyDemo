/02 — Recovery Wing
/02 — Recover

Recovery
is the work.

The recovery wing isn't an afterthought. It's a separate, sequenced experience — built for the protocol-followers and the curious-first-timers alike. Bookable from your phone. Quiet. Premium. Done right.

Cold

Cold Plunge

3 dedicated plunges, dialed between 38–42°F. Filtered, sanitized, ready when you are.

Protocol 2–5 min · Post-lift
Hot

Cedar Sauna

180°F dry cedar sauna. Authentic Finnish-style, built for true heat exposure — not a glorified closet.

Protocol 15–20 min · 3–4×/wk
Heat

Infrared Sauna

Full-spectrum infrared. Lower temps, deeper penetration. Solo or two-person sessions.

Protocol 30–45 min · Recovery days
Light

Red Light Beds

Medical-grade full-body red + near-infrared light. Skin, joints, mitochondria. Stack with sauna.

Protocol 10–20 min · Daily
Compression

Normatec Suites

Full-leg + arm + hip pneumatic compression. Lounge chairs, dim lights, headphones provided.

Protocol 30 min · Post-training
Private

Private Suite

Plunge + sauna + light, all yours for an hour. The Elite tier perk that actually feels luxe.

Protocol 60 min · Bookable
