Questions

The fine print.

Is it really free?

Yes. No card on file, no automatic rollover into a membership, nothing to cancel. At the end of the seven days you decide whether to join — and if you don't, that's the end of it.

Do I need to sign up or book first?

If you already submitted a free-pass form, including on Facebook or Instagram, you don't need to submit again. Bring your email or form confirmation and share the email address you used with the front desk. No appointment needed. See how to redeem your pass.

Can I train the same day I walk in?

Yes — that's the point. Bring shoes and a water bottle and you can train from that first visit. No waiting period, no appointment, and no scheduled tour first.

Does the pass include recovery?

It does. Cold plunge, cedar and infrared sauna, red light therapy, and Normatec compression are all included for the week, same as the training floor. We’re mid-build on the dedicated recovery space, so for now it’s all set up on the main floor.

When do my seven days start?

Only when you visit REVIVE and redeem your pass at the front desk. Submitting a form or receiving your email does not start the clock. Your pass runs for seven consecutive days from redemption. There is no specific claim deadline.

What does a membership cost after?

Three tiers, one price each: Essential $89/mo, Plus $139/mo, Elite $169/mo. Month-to-month, no contract, no initiation fee. See what's in each tier.
