Questions

The fine print.

Is it really free?

Yes. No card on file, no automatic rollover into a membership, nothing to cancel. At the end of the seven days you decide whether to join — and if you don't, that's the end of it.

Do I have to reserve online first?

No. Reserving just means your pass is ready and we're expecting you. You can walk in during opening hours and claim it at the front desk instead.

Can I work out the day I claim it?

Yes — that's the point. Bring shoes, a water bottle, and a photo ID, and you can train from that first visit. No waiting period and no scheduled tour first.

Does the pass include the recovery wing?

It does. Cold plunge, cedar and infrared sauna, red light therapy, and Normatec compression are all included for the week, same as the training floor.

When does the offer end?

Passes must be claimed by Monday, August 31. Your seven days run from the day you claim it, so a pass claimed on the 31st still runs into September.

What does a membership cost after?

Three tiers, one price each: Essential $89/mo, Plus $139/mo, Elite $169/mo. Month-to-month, no contract, no initiation fee. See what's in each tier.
