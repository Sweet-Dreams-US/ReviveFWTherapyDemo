Every gym runs an opening promotion. Most of them are the same discount with a new date stapled on. REVIVE's founding offer is a little different, and the difference is worth understanding before it closes on Saturday, August 15.

Founders pricing is not a sale. It is a rate you keep. Here is exactly what that means, and what happens the day the doors open.

## What founders pricing actually is

Join before the grand opening on August 15 and the rate you sign up at is the rate you pay for as long as your membership stays active. Not for a promotional year. For the life of the membership.

The founding rates are **$79 a month for Base, $109 for Plus, and $149 for Elite.** After August 15, those become $89, $139, and $179 — the standard rates the gym runs from then on. As REVIVE grows and adds to the space, prices will move. Founders do not move with them. That is the whole deal: you trade acting early for a rate that never climbs.

It is month-to-month, with no long-term contract. You can cancel any time. The only thing you cannot do is get the founding rate back once it closes.

## Why it's a deadline, not a coupon

A coupon knocks a few dollars off your first few months and then quietly expires. This is the opposite. There is no promo window that runs out and no rate that creeps back up later. Whatever tier you lock before August 15, you hold at that price for as long as you keep the membership.

The offer is open to anyone who joins before the grand opening — there is no waitlist to clear and no spot to compete for. The only thing standing between you and the founding rate is the date.

Honest version: a lifetime price lock is only worth acting on if you actually plan to train here. If you are not sure REVIVE fits your routine, come to the grand opening first, use the recovery suite, walk the floor, and decide from there. The deadline is real, but so is the value of not paying for something you will not use.

## What you lock in besides the price

The rate is the headline, but it is not the only thing that closes on the 15th.

Founding members get the full recovery suite as part of how REVIVE works, not as an upsell — cold plunge, cedar and infrared saunas, red light therapy, and Normatec compression, bookable from your phone. They get the free Saturday run club, which is open to the whole city but built by the members who show up. And they get first access during opening week, before the general public.

None of that is a limited-time trick. It is simply the version of REVIVE that exists for the people who were here at the start.

## How to lock it in before it closes

Two ways, and both end at the same moment.

You can reserve your rate now at revivefw.com — pick your tier, get on the list, and the team confirms your founding rate before the deadline. Or you can walk in on grand opening day, August 15, and sign up on site, where **enrollment fees are waived** for anyone who joins that day.

Either way, Saturday, August 15 is the line. Before it, you lock a rate for life. After it, you join at standard pricing — still welcome, just a little later and a little more.

## The Revive Tip

If you are on the fence, do the five-year math before the 15th, not after. Take the tier you would actually use — most people land on Plus — and multiply the monthly gap between the founding rate and the standard rate across the years you plan to train in Fort Wayne. For a lot of people that difference pays for a few months of membership on its own. Then decide. You can reserve your founding rate and see what each tier includes on [the membership page](https://revivefw.com/join) before the deadline closes it.
