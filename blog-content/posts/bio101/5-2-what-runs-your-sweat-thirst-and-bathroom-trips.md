You drink a liter during a hard session and never need the bathroom. You drink the same liter at your desk and you are up twice before lunch.

Same water, different answer. Four systems make that call, and most are stranger than people picture.

## The filter that decides how much water you keep

Your kidneys sit behind the abdominal cavity against the back wall, level with the lowest ribs — the right one slightly lower, because the liver is in the way. Inside is an outer **cortex** and inner **medulla** in pyramids, draining into calyces and the renal pelvis.

The working unit is the **nephron**, about a million per kidney. It starts with the **glomerulus**, a knot of capillaries inside a cup called Bowman's capsule, which filters plasma out of the blood. That filtrate runs through the proximal tubule, down the **loop of Henle** into the salty medulla, back up the distal tubule and into a collecting duct, reclaiming most of the water and salt.

That descent into the medulla is the trick that lets you concentrate urine. When you are losing fluid to sweat, hormonal signals dial up how much gets reabsorbed — the actual answer to the liter question.

From there urine runs down the **ureters** to the **bladder** and out through the **urethra**: short in women, longer in men where it passes through the prostate.

Blood in the urine, pain with urination, or a sudden change in output is a physician's question.

## The glands are smaller and more scattered than you think

The **pituitary** is pea-sized, tucked in a bony pocket at the skull base called the sella turcica. The **thyroid** wraps the front of the trachea below the larynx like a butterfly, with four rice-grain-sized **parathyroid** glands on its back surface. The **adrenals** cap each kidney, an outer cortex and inner medulla that are effectively two glands in one. The **pancreatic islets** are scattered clusters inside the pancreas, the **gonads** sit in the pelvis or scrotum, and the **pineal** is deep in the brain.

### The label that is not a diagnosis

"Adrenal fatigue" is not a recognized medical diagnosis. It has been reviewed and rejected by endocrinology bodies.

That does not make the symptoms imaginary. Exhaustion, poor sleep and brain fog are worth investigating — but real adrenal and thyroid disorders are diagnosed with blood work by a physician, and a supplement stack sold against a made-up label just delays that. If this has gone on for months, get tested.

## The muscle group nobody trains and everyone uses

The **pelvic floor** is a sling of muscle — mainly levator ani and coccygeus — closing the bottom of the pelvic bowl, with openings for the urethra, rectum, and in women the vagina. The reproductive organs sit above and through it: uterus, ovaries and tubes in the female pelvis; prostate and seminal vesicles in the male, with the testes outside the body cavity because sperm production needs cooler temperatures.

In a gym, the floor works with the diaphragm above, transversus abdominis around and the deep spinal muscles behind, as one pressure system. When you brace, it is part of the brace.

Leaking during squats, box jumps or a run is extremely common, and common is not the same as normal or permanent. Pelvic floor physical therapy is a real specialty, and kegels are not automatically the answer — some presentations get worse with more squeezing. That is an evaluation, not a guess, and it applies to men too, particularly after prostate surgery.

## What your skin is doing in the sauna

The **epidermis** is the outer layer and has no blood supply of its own. Cells divide at the bottom in the stratum basale, migrate up, fill with keratin, die, and flake off the stratum corneum.

Beneath it the **dermis** holds collagen and elastin, blood vessels, nerve endings, hair follicles and sweat glands. Below that, the **hypodermis** is fat and loose connective tissue. **Eccrine** sweat glands cover nearly the whole body and handle temperature; **apocrine** glands in the armpit and groin switch on after puberty.

Here is the mechanism people get backwards. Sweat does nothing while it is liquid. Cooling happens only when it **evaporates** — which is why a humid Fort Wayne July is harder than a dry ninety degrees, and why sweat dripping off you is fluid lost without much cooling bought. Meanwhile dermal vessels widen to bring warm blood to the surface, the flush you feel in the sauna and part of why your heart rate climbs sitting still.

One thing sweat is not: a meaningful route for eliminating anything besides water and salt. That belongs to the kidneys and liver. The sauna is worth doing on its own merits.
