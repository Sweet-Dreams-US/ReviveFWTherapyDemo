Questions

The fine print.

What is founders pricing?

Join before Grand Opening on August 15 and you lock the founders rate — $79 / $109 / $149 — for life. As long as your membership stays active, you never pay standard rates.

What happens after August 15?

Standard pricing applies — $89 / $139 / $179. Founders pricing closes at Grand Opening and won't reopen. Ever.

When do you actually open?

Grand Opening is Saturday, August 15 — with opening-week access for waitlist members first. Join the waitlist and you'll get the details by email.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No long-term contract. Memberships are month-to-month. Cancel any time, no fees. Founders pricing stays active as long as your membership is uninterrupted.

How do I book recovery sessions?

Through the Revive member app or web portal. Plus members get a 24-hour booking window. Elite gets 72-hour priority — book your week's recovery on Friday and walk in at the time you want.

Can I bring a guest?

Elite members get 2 guest passes per month. Plus and Base members can bring guests for $25/visit. Run club is open to anyone — even non-members — twice a week.

What if I want to upgrade later?

Easy — upgrade anytime through the member app. Your founders pricing scales with you: joined at Founder Base ($79)? Upgrading to Plus locks in Founder Plus pricing ($109), not standard.
