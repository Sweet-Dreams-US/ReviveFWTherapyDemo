Questions

The fine print.

How much is a membership?

Three tiers, one price each: Essential $89/mo, Plus $139/mo, Elite $169/mo. Month-to-month, no contract, no initiation fee.

Can I try the gym before I join?

Yes. You can get a free 7-day pass — full access to the training floor and recovery for a week, no card required. Show your free-pass email or form confirmation and share the email you used to claim at the front desk. Your seven days start when you redeem, not when you submit a form. Here's how it works.

What's the September offer?

Through September, join on an Elite membership and your first personal training session with Kings Nutrition is included, free. We'll book it with you when you sign up.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No long-term contract. Memberships are month-to-month. Cancel any time, no fees.

How do I book recovery sessions?

Through the Revive member app or web portal. Plus members get a 24-hour booking window. Elite gets 72-hour priority — book your week's recovery on Friday and walk in at the time you want.

Can I bring a guest?

Elite members get 2 guest passes per month. Plus and Essential members can bring guests for $25/visit. Run club is free and open to anyone — members and non-members.

What if I want to upgrade later?

Easy — upgrade anytime through the member app. The new rate starts on your next billing date, and there's no upgrade fee.
